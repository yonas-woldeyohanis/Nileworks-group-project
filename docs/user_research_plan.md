# Hunarly — User Research Plan

## 1. Overview

**Project:** Hunarly — Job & Internship Finder for Ethiopian Students  
**Platform:** Mobile Application (React Native / Expo)  
**Target Region:** Ethiopia (primary markets: Addis Ababa, Jimma, Hawassa, Bahir Dar)  
**Research Period:** 4 weeks

---

## 2. Research Objectives

1. Understand the current job-search behaviors of Ethiopian university students.
2. Identify the pain points students face when looking for internships and entry-level jobs.
3. Understand what employers need when recruiting fresh graduates or interns locally.
4. Validate core feature assumptions: job listings, application tracking, messaging, and profile building.
5. Assess smartphone and internet usage patterns to inform technical design decisions.

---

## 3. Research Questions

### Students
- How do students currently find internship and job opportunities?
- What channels do they use (word of mouth, notice boards, social media, LinkedIn)?
- What information do they most want to see on a job listing?
- What barriers exist when applying for jobs (CV, language, internet access, fear of rejection)?
- How important is seeing the application status after applying?
- Would students trust a mobile app to manage job applications?

### Employers
- How do employers currently recruit fresh graduates and interns in Ethiopia?
- What qualities do employers look for in early-career candidates?
- How painful is the applicant screening process?
- What information do they need from a student's profile to make a decision?
- What communication channels do employers prefer with candidates?

---

## 4. Methodology

### 4.1 User Interviews (Primary)
- **Participants:** 8–12 students from 3–4 universities; 4–6 HR managers or business owners
- **Format:** Semi-structured, 30–45 minutes, in-person or Google Meet
- **Language:** Amharic and English (bilingual guide prepared)
- **Recruitment:** University career centers, LinkedIn, personal networks

### 4.2 Survey (Secondary)
- **Tool:** Google Forms
- **Participants:** 50–100 university students
- **Distribution:** WhatsApp groups, Telegram channels, campus notice boards
- **Questions:** 12–15 questions covering job search behavior, app usage, pain points

### 4.3 Contextual Observation
- Visit 2 university career centers and observe how students interact with physical job boards and career advisors.
- Note: types of postings, student engagement, questions asked.

### 4.4 Competitive Analysis
- Evaluate existing platforms students use: LinkedIn, Telegram job channels, EthioJobs, Facebook groups.
- Assess gaps in features, usability, local relevance.

---

## 5. Participant Profiles

| Profile | Criteria |
|---------|----------|
| Student A | 1st–2nd year, tech-savvy, active on social media |
| Student B | 3rd–4th year, actively job hunting, has applied before |
| Student C | Graduate student or recent graduate, employed or unemployed |
| Employer A | SME hiring 1–10 people/year, non-tech sector |
| Employer B | Mid-size company (tech, finance, NGO), structured HR process |

---

## 6. Interview Guide (Students)

**Introduction (5 min)**
- Brief about the research; confirm consent; explain anonymity.

**Background (5 min)**
- What year are you? What are you studying?
- Do you have a smartphone? What apps do you use most?

**Job Search Behavior (10 min)**
- Have you looked for a job or internship before? Walk me through that process.
- Where did you look? What worked? What didn't?
- How did you find out about opportunities?

**Pain Points (10 min)**
- What was the hardest part of the process?
- Did you ever apply and hear nothing back? How did that feel?
- How do you feel about sharing your CV or personal details online?

**Feature Reactions (10 min)**
- Show low-fidelity wireframes of Hunarly. Ask: "What do you see here? What would you do first?"
- Ask about: job filtering, application tracker, chat with employer.

**Closing (5 min)**
- Is there anything else about job hunting that frustrates you?
- What would make a job app truly useful to you?

---

## 7. Analysis Plan

- Transcribe and translate interviews (Amharic → English where needed)
- Code themes using affinity mapping (physical sticky notes or Miro)
- Identify top 5 pain points and top 5 motivators per user group
- Create 2–3 user personas from synthesized findings
- Update feature backlog based on validated needs

## 8. Timeline

| Week | Activity |
|------|----------|
| Week 1 | Recruit participants; finalize interview guide; launch survey |
| Week 2 | Conduct student interviews (6–8) |
| Week 3 | Conduct employer interviews (4–6); contextual observation |
| Week 4 | Transcribe, analyze, synthesize; write research summary |

---

## 9. Ethical Considerations

- All participants provide verbal or written informed consent.
- No personally identifiable information stored in research data.
- Participants may withdraw at any time without consequence.
- Audio recordings deleted after transcription.
- Data stored securely; access limited to research team.

---

## 10. Expected Outcomes

- 2–3 validated user personas
- Ranked list of top pain points per audience segment
- Feature validation matrix (confirmed / rejected / modified)
- Design recommendations for MVP scope
- Input for the system workflow map and architecture decisions
